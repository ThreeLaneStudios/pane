# Code of conduct

Pane is a small project with a small community. The rule is short: be decent.

Argue about the work, not the person. Disagree with a scope, a design, or a ruling as hard as you like, with evidence; do not attack whoever wrote it. No harassment, no slurs, no doxxing, no sexual content, and no pile-ons, in the issue tracker, in pull requests, or on the Discord. Assume the other person is trying to make Pane better, because they almost always are.

The maintainer's call is final on what gets built and what gets merged. You can make the case again later with new information; you cannot relitigate it in every thread.

If someone crosses the line, email [admin@paneapp.ai](mailto:admin@paneapp.ai) with a link to what happened. A person reads it. Depending on what it is, the response is a warning, a removed comment, or a ban from the repo and the Discord. Reports are kept private.

This applies to everyone, including the maintainer and the agents.
