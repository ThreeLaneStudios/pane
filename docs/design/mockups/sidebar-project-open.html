Passat Overview

GeneralUpdated 2 hours ago1,226 chars

The Volkswagen Passat is a midsize sedan that has been in production since the 1970s, known for its solid build quality, refined driving dynamics, and practical design.

Key Features

  • Available in sedan and wagon body styles across global markets
  • Turbocharged engine options ranging from efficient four-cylinders to powerful V6 configurations
  • Advanced driver assistance features including adaptive cruise control and lane keeping

Color Options

Current model year offerings include Pure White, Platinum Gray Metallic, Aurora Red Metallic, Tourmaline Blue Metallic, and Deep Black Pearl Effect.

The trunk capacity of 15.9 cubic feet makes it one of the most practical options in its segment.
