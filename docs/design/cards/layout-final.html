Trunk

The trunk is one of the defining features of the Volkswagen Beetle, known for its front-mounted trunk or "frunk" design that set it apart from virtually every other car on the road.

Unlike conventional rear-engine layouts, the Beetle's air-cooled flat-four engine sat in the back, freeing up the front compartment for storage. This arrangement gave the car its distinctive rounded hood and created a surprisingly practical cargo area.

The original trunk design remained largely unchanged from 1938 through the final Mexican-built Beetles in 2003, making it one of the longest-running automotive design elements in history.
